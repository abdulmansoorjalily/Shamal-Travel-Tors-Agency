About Us – Shamal Travel & Tours

Shamal Travel & Tours is a premier travel consultancy and service provider based in Kabul, Afghanistan, with branch offices in Dubai. Since our establishment in 2008, we have been delivering reliable and comprehensive travel solutions, including international and domestic air ticketing, visa facilitation, and customized tour packages.

Our experienced team is committed to providing smooth and stress-free travel experiences tailored to each client’s needs. Built on trust, efficiency, and personalized service, we have earned a strong reputation as a dependable name in the travel industry. Whether you are planning a business trip, family holiday, or group tour, we ensure every detail is managed with care and precision.

We value your feedback and continuously strive to improve our services. Your suggestions are always welcome—please feel free to contact us anytime.
